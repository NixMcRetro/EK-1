EK-1 v1.0.1 - offline release

1. Extract the entire ZIP (do not open the HTML inside a ZIP preview).
2. Double-click index.html. Keep the other files and folders beside it.
3. Play the keyboard or use Song Builder. All panels start open; collapse
   any you do not need. Appearance (System, Light, Dark) and panel states
   are remembered when local browser storage is available. No server or launcher is required.
   Note names are printed on the keyboard rails. Comma and period shift the
   computer keyboard shortcuts down/up one octave. C plays A3, V plays B3,
   B plays middle C. PAUSE/RESUME freezes or continues playback. MIDI playback colours
   show channels, splitting the key when several channels share a note.
4. Optional: download FluidR3 GM via the Sound Library link, or use your
   own SF2/SF3 file. Click OPEN SOUNDFONT. No sound banks are bundled.
5. Press DEMO to hear the five original songs with your loaded SoundFont.
   The selected demo repeats until stopped. Uncheck Loop demonstration song
   in Sound options to play it once.
   MELODY OFF lets you play along; STOP also silences held sample notes.
   BACKING VOLUME starts at 80% and balances demo backing and auto accompaniment.
   With a SoundFont loaded, auto accompaniment uses sampled chords, bass and
   percussion (built-in drums if the bank has no drum presets).
6. Choose Natural or Expressive feel in Song Builder for more musical
   grooves. Original keeps the earlier arrangement engine.
7. NEW MELODY keeps the chords; NEW CHORDS + MELODY replaces both.
   PLAY SONG returns to the keyboard unless you uncheck the option.
   Main volume starts at 50%. Keep device/headphone volume low when testing.
8. Use the Song Structure row to select or duplicate a section, and Undo/Redo
   to recover edits. Expand Sound options for favourites and optional effects.
9. Save your song as an editable .ek1 project, MIDI, WAV or MP3.
