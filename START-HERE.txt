EK-1 v0.9.3 - offline release candidate

1. Extract the entire ZIP (do not open the HTML inside a ZIP preview).
2. Double-click index.html. Keep the other files and folders beside it.
3. Play the keyboard or open Song Builder. No server or launcher is required.
4. Optional: download FluidR3 GM via the Sound Library link, or use your
   own SF2/SF3 file. Click OPEN SOUNDFONT. No sound banks are bundled.
5. Press DEMO to hear the five original songs with your loaded SoundFont.
   MELODY OFF lets you play along; STOP also silences held sample notes.
6. Choose Natural or Expressive feel in Song Builder for more musical
   grooves. Original keeps the earlier arrangement engine.
7. NEW MELODY keeps the chords; NEW CHORDS + MELODY replaces both.
   PLAY SONG returns to the keyboard unless you uncheck the option.
   Main volume starts at 25%. Keep device/headphone volume low when testing.
8. Save your song as an editable .ek1 project, MIDI, WAV or MP3.
