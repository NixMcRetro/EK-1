# 🎹 Electronic Keyboard (Model EK-1)

**A little bit of 1990s keyboard nostalgia, right in your browser.**

No downloads needed to play online. No installation, accounts, or music theory required. Just pick a voice and make some noise! 🎶

[**▶ Play on McRetro.net**](https://mcretro.net/goodies/ek-1/) | [**📦 Download v1.0.1**](https://github.com/NixMcRetro/EK-1/releases/tag/v1.0.1)

## ✨ What's inside?

| | |
| --- | --- |
| 🎹 **Keyboard** | 49 keys, 12 Classic voices, and 8 six-operator FM voices |
| 🎛️ **SoundFonts** | Bring your own SF2 or SF3 instrument library |
| 🥁 **Auto accompaniment** | Six rhythm styles, bass, chords, and backing volume |
| 🎵 **Song Bank** | Five original looping demos to play along with |
| 🌈 **MIDI player** | Colour-coded notes, looping, and voice overrides |
| 🎼 **Song Builder** | Generate and edit melodies, chords, bass, and drums |
| 💾 **Save your music** | Editable projects, MIDI, WAV, and MP3 |
| 🌙 **Make it yours** | Light and dark modes, effects, and remembered layout |

## 🚀 Play offline

1. [Download the offline ZIP](https://github.com/NixMcRetro/EK-1/releases/download/v1.0.1/EK-1-v1.0.1-offline.zip).
2. Extract the whole ZIP.
3. Double-click `index.html` and play!

Keep all the supporting files together. Your computer keyboard works too: **B** is middle C, and comma / period shift the octave.

🎧 Main volume starts at 50%. Keep your device or headphone volume low when getting started.

## 🎻 More sounds, please!

Try [**FluidR3 GM**](https://www.pianobooster.org/download.html), a free SoundFont by Frank Wen (MIT licence, approximately 148 MB). Download it separately and select **OPEN SOUNDFONT** in EK-1.

Your SoundFont stays on your device. No sound banks are bundled or automatically downloaded.

## 🎶 Make a song!

Pick Pop, Rock, Disco, Ska, Reggae, or Electronic in Song Builder, then generate an arrangement. Change the chords, edit the melody, adjust the mix, and export your masterpiece. No need to start with an empty score!
