Choose which melodic channels follow VOICE. Changes apply to the next notes, without restarting playback.

Key colours identify MIDI channels. Shared pitches show split colours; percussion and out-of-range notes are not lit.

SONG BUILDER Make your own music

Choose a style, generate an arrangement, and edit any chord or melody note. Play it back or save a MIDI file.

Key, chord pattern and length are applied by NEW ARRANGEMENT, which replaces the current notes. Style, tempo and feel apply at playback. Natural adds subtle groove; Expressive adds stronger variation; Original retains the earlier arrangement engine.
